# LightekMCG network launch kit

## The public introduction

> Lightek Media & Communications Group is building a Black-led media network where creators can turn a show and a community into a business. Fans help shape the programming by sharing clips and telling creators what they want to see. Creators can build a crew and a catalog, bring in sponsors, and pitch programming to television partners.

**Campaign line** — Start with a show. Build a media company.

**TV campaign line** — Make it here. Put it on TV.

**Short description** — A Black-led media network bringing community, production, and television distribution together.

**Agency offer** — Build your business on Lightek for $200 a year. Serve clients under your brand, build your own packages from Lightek services, set client prices, and manage the features you offer. Bring client programming, carriage, and sponsorship opportunities into the network. Any rights and revenue shares for those opportunities are set in the relevant agreement.

## Copy for a one-sheet or event flyer

### Make it here. Put it on TV.

What do you wish you could watch on TV? Who in your city is already making it?

Lightek is building a Black-led network for independent films, local shows, sports, and live events. Here’s how to get involved.

- **Watch** — Ask for lineup updates. Tell us what belongs on the guide.
- **Create** — Bring a series, film, or live event and the community you serve. Explore paid production, licensing, and sponsorship.
- **Bring a station** — Explore carrying your channel on Lightek TV while you keep running your station.
- **Sponsor** — Discuss getting your business on air around a show, event, or participating channel.
- **Join a crew** — Send a reel or portfolio to introduce yourself for future production and broadcast work.
- **Build an agency** — For $200 a year, use Lightek to package services, set your prices, and serve clients under your brand.

Email **hello@lightekmcg.com** with the way you’d like to get involved. Send this to someone whose work deserves an audience.

## Newsroom and press

**Suggested headline** — LightekMCG invites independent creators and stations to help build a Black-led television network

**For a newsroom post or media pitch**

Lightek Media & Communications Group is inviting independent creators, stations, production professionals, local sponsors, agencies, and viewers to help shape its television network. The company is preparing a channel guide for the web and an Octavia viewing experience on Roku. The network is designed to bring Lightek programming and independently operated stations into one lineup, with opportunities for programming licenses and advertising partnerships.

For interviews or partnership inquiries, contact **hello@lightekmcg.com**. Creators can send a trailer or a project summary. Stations can share their market and public stream or website. Viewers can request updates and suggest programs they want to see. Agencies can ask about the $200-a-year plan to serve clients under their own brand.

## What each person gets and what happens next

| Audience | What they can get from Lightek | First reply to send |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Viewers | A place to discover local and independent programming and influence the lineup | Thanks for reaching out. What city are you watching from, and what station or show would you like to see? |
| Creators | Distribution and possible licensing, paid production, or sponsorship arrangements | Send a trailer or short description, the intended audience, and whether you’re seeking production support, distribution, or both. We’ll discuss the fit and terms before requesting a full delivery. |
| Stations | Another outlet for the channel, potential syndication and sponsored-airing revenue, and viewership reporting | Send your market, public stream or website, and programming focus. We can discuss carriage, reporting, inventory, and any proposed revenue share. |
| Sponsors | A specific programming placement with defined deliverables and reporting | Tell us your market, campaign objective, timing, and budget range. We’ll discuss available placements and what we can report. |
| Crew and talent | A route to introduce themselves for paid work when projects are staffed | Send your role, market, reel, and availability. If a project fits, we’ll discuss scope, rate, and timing before booking. |
| Agencies | White-label packages, client pricing and feature controls, and a route to pitch network opportunities | Tell us which services you sell, the clients you serve, and the first package you want to offer. We’ll walk through the $200 annual plan and how to set up your first client. |

## First campaign handoff

Use the network landing page as the common destination. Share the launch introduction through Lightek’s own channels, send personalized invitations to stations and creators, and take the one-sheet to local events. The audience pages at `/media` use distinct email subjects to label replies. Assign someone to acknowledge each reply and record the contact, market, offer or ask, and next action. Review which invitations led to a program pitch, carriage conversation, sponsorship brief, crew introduction, agency plan inquiry, or request to watch.

The first public story to tell once it happens is a real one. Name the station, the program, the people who made it, where viewers can see it, and who sponsored it, with permission. Once a payment has settled and the parties agree to share it, explain how the program or sponsored spot generated compensation.

## Publication settings

- Confirm that hello@lightekmcg.com receives mail and assign an owner for replies before promoting the pages. The inquiry buttons open the visitor’s email app with a subject and prompts. The agency page shows the existing demo link when demo intake is enabled for that host.
- Update the viewing copy with the web guide and Roku links when both destinations are available to the intended audience. Keep the updates and programming suggestions route alongside them.
- Use the written deal to set rights, rates, eligibility, reporting, and any revenue split. Publish specific partner names, dates, audience figures, payout results, or Nielsen certification claims once verified and cleared for publication.
