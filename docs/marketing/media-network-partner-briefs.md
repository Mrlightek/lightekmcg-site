# Lightek Media partner briefs

Collect the answers before drafting a proposal or starting work. Record unresolved points with an owner and due date. Use current commercial and rights agreements for signatures.

## Creator and production

- **Project and audience** — Working title, format, episode length, market, audience or community link, trailer, and production status.
- **Operating plan** — Crew, budget, episode schedule, production and distribution support requested, and the next deliverable.
- **Rights and clearance** — Rights holder, contributors, releases, music and archival clearances, permitted outlets and territory, term, and permissions for clips, replay and syndication.
- **Compensation and delivery** — Proposed fee, milestone or sponsorship split, invoice recipient, delivery specifications, accessibility materials, and approval owner.

## Affiliate station and distribution

- **Station and market** — Station, operator, service area, public URL, programming focus, and technical contact.
- **Carriage and rights** — Outlet and territory, licensed programs and windows, who manages promotion, and who controls advertising inventory.
- **Playback and operations** — Authorized feed details through the approved channel, expected schedule, fallback, blackout windows, and incident contact.
- **Reporting and settlement** — Proof of airing and reporting cadence, commercial split if any, payment terms, and who signs off delivery.

## Sponsor and advertiser

- **Campaign** — Buyer, market, audience, goal, timing, budget, and creative contact.
- **Inventory** — Show or event, channel or outlet, number and duration of placements, creative deadline, approval owner, and makegood terms.
- **Price and proof** — Quoted amount, billing milestones, delivery report, and measurements available for the booking. Agree on which delivery evidence the buyer will accept.
- **Settlement** — Record the delivered placements, reconcile any makegoods or adjustments, and record applicable partner payment status through Dymond Bank.

## Agency discovery and first client

- **Offer** — $200 annual agency plan, expected services, who the agency serves, and the brand it will present to clients.
- **First package** — Client and use case, package features and limits, underlying service costs, agency sale price, and who delivers ongoing support.
- **First delivery** — Existing demo if available, account admin, first client setup, and the task the client should be able to complete.
- **Media opportunity** — Identify an actual show, station, or sponsor that the client wants to bring into the network. Scope any programming rights, placement, or split with the relevant owners and agreements.

## Crew intake

Ask for the person’s location, specialty, role on the work in their reel, travel or remote availability, and potential conflicts. For a staffed project, put scope, dates, credits, rate, and payment timing in the engagement.
