# Lightek Media network launch playbook

Start with the communities that already gather around local sports, films, events, or independent reporting. The first campaign should produce a viable program pitch and a station conversation, plus a reason for viewers to return after the premiere.

## How the network grows

A creator asks a community what it wants to see. People share clips and talk about the work. The creator turns that interest into a recurring show, lines up crew and a sponsor, and pitches an outlet. The show can bring viewers back to the community and give them a reason to follow the next episode. Agencies can offer sites, production, and other services to several businesses at once. Stations can bring a lineup and an existing audience. Those businesses expand what Lightek can offer alongside Lightek’s own productions.

## Run the first campaign

1. Choose one market and programming idea that already has signs of audience interest. Invite creators and stations with a specific reason they fit. Use the public pages as their destination.
2. Ask viewers for one show, station, or event they want covered. Use those responses and public community signals to refine the pitch.
3. Scope the pilot with the creator and station. Record rights and operational requirements. If a sponsor fits, issue a proposal with dates, deliverables, price, and reporting.
4. After all parties agree, schedule the work and verify the public destinations from a viewer’s device. Have the operator confirm the stream, fallback programming, and who responds to an outage.
5. Verify the airing and reconcile any sponsorship delivery. Use the existing Dymond Bank workflow for eligible paid arrangements and record settlement evidence.
6. With permission, share the resulting show, creators, station, and sponsor in the newsroom. Invite viewers to follow the next episode and suggest another story.

Launch measures should distinguish viewing requests, qualified project pitches, station conversations, agency demos, signed agreements, aired programs, verified sponsorship delivery, and settled payments. Record the source of each outcome.
