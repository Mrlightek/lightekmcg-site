# Lightek Sales & Marketing Kit

First campaign: managed websites for local service businesses. Suggested starting audience: home-service owners who already receive referrals and want a clearer way for those prospects to see what they do and request an estimate. Use one trade in one market for the first campaign so you can compare responses to the same offer.

## Brand and offer

**Lightek company description.** Lightek Media & Communications Group develops software and services for businesses, agencies, and creators. Its work spans websites and business operations, communications, and media production and distribution.

**First offer.** A Lightek managed website for a service business. The sales conversation focuses on making services, service areas, and the next step easy to find. The written proposal defines the pages, inquiry form, hosting and support included in the package.

**Rep’s 30-second pitch.** “When someone gets your name from a referral, they usually want to know three things before they call: Do you do this kind of work? Do you come to my area? How do I get an estimate? We can show you a website organized around those questions and walk through where the inquiry goes. If it fits, we’ll put the scope and recurring cost in writing.”

**Offer details to confirm before publication**

| Detail | Approved answer needed |
|---|---|
| Catalog package | Product name, included pages, hosting, support and update responsibilities |
| Price | Setup fee, recurring price, billing interval and approved extras |
| Delivery | What the customer supplies, when work starts, expected timeline and handoff |
| Ownership and exit | Domain, content, account access, cancellation and migration terms |
| Demo | One working sample site, one tested inquiry flow, and one confirmed booking link |

**Two-week campaign**

Publish the demo landing page and one LinkedIn post in week one. Send individually reviewed outreach to businesses in the same trade and market. In week two, publish the walkthrough video and follow up on open conversations. Use a tagged link for each placement and review demo requests, demos held, quotes and paid accounts by source. For the first copy test, compare “Make it easy to request an estimate” with “Show customers where you work and how to reach you.” Keep the offer and booking flow consistent.

## Rep playbook

### Run a 15-minute demo

| Time | Rep action |
|---|---|
| 0–4 minutes | Ask where referrals come from, how people request estimates today, who responds, and what details they need. |
| 4–10 minutes | Open the sample site on a phone. Find the service, confirm the service area, submit a synthetic estimate request, and show where it arrives. |
| 10–13 minutes | Connect what the buyer saw to their actual process. Confirm the pages and handoff they need, identify who approves a purchase, and flag anything outside the standard package. |
| 13–15 minutes | Agree on the next step and date. For a qualified buyer, confirm what the quote will cover and when you’ll review it together. Log the decision in Sales & CRM. |

### Six-slide pitch deck copy

| Slide | On-screen copy | What to show |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Turn a recommendation into an estimate request | A mobile view of the sample business site |
| 2 | The three questions customers ask | What do you do? Do you serve my area? How do I get an estimate? |
| 3 | What your customer sees | Homepage, service page and estimate button |
| 4 | What your team sees | A labeled test inquiry and its assigned follow-up |
| 5 | What’s included | The approved package, who does what, and the price and billing interval |
| 6 | Review the package for your business | The agreed scope and next meeting date |

### Answer common objections

- **“We already get referrals.”** “How do those referrals check your work and contact you? Let’s look at that step and see if there’s anything worth changing.”
- **“We already have a website.”** “Let’s start with the site you have. Show me how you’d request an estimate on your phone. If that already works well, we can focus on any other gap you mentioned.”
- **“How much is it?”** “I’ll show you the published package and put the setup and recurring charges in the quote. We’ll price any extra work before you agree to it.”
- **“Can you guarantee more jobs?”** “We can show and test the inquiry process. Job volume depends on how people find you and what happens after they contact you.”
- **“Who owns the website and the domain?”** “Let’s review the ownership, access and cancellation terms for the package together before you sign.”

### Hand off a paid sale

Record the accepted scope, paid order reference, buyer and onboarding contact, domain owner, materials still needed, assigned onboarding owner, and date of the next customer update. Add that update to the deal so the rep can answer questions after close. Capture the rep’s credit when the commission workflow is available.

## Build the next set of materials

The same structure can support the rest of Lightek. Give each offer its own buyer, pitch, landing page, demo, written scope, and proof. Keep the company story consistent across them.

| Offer | Sales material to build | Proof to gather |
|---|---|---|
| Managed websites | Publish the first campaign and train the first rep with this kit. | A complete sample inquiry and approved customer results when available. |
| Managed IT | Service overview, coverage matrix, discovery questionnaire, and onboarding walkthrough. | Verified support workflows, coverage terms and service commitments. |
| Business communications | One comparison for CMS only, new customer bundle, and existing customer upgrade. | Supported locations, published pricing and verified activation steps for the phone and data services. |
| Agency and white label | Partner deck, reseller economics, permissions guide, and client demo. | A working branded workspace and purchase-to-provisioning walkthrough. |
| Studio and creator media | Creator reel, workflow demo, and network or affiliate overview. | A verified path from creation to publication and documented rights and monetization terms. |

## Give each piece an owner

| Owner | Recurring responsibility |
|---|---|
| CEO | Approve positioning, offer and pricing. Review outcomes and decide which segment or offer to expand. |
| Sales lead or pilot rep | Maintain the discovery questions, run demos, own the next action, and report recurring objections and lost-deal reasons. |
| Nevaeh and marketing | Prepare campaign drafts, maintain the approved copy, surface overdue follow-ups, and assemble the weekly results using configured permissions. |
| Delivery and support | Confirm what customers receive, verify the demo and handoff, and document customer results with permission. |
| Finance | Reconcile paid orders and commission credit, and approve exceptions and payouts. |

At the weekly review, compare qualified demos held, quotes issued, paid and activated customers, and margin after commission. Keep one current copy of each asset. Record its owner, revision date, approved claims, and the catalog offer it describes. Update it when price, scope or delivery changes.
