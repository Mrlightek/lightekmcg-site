Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

This policy describes how Lightek Media & Communications Group (LightekMCG, “Lightek”) handles information for its website, community, hosted applications, media publishing and related services. Independently operated customer websites may have additional policies identifying their operator.

Information we handle

  • Account and profile information you provide, including your name, email address, profile details and account-security settings.
  • Content you submit, including posts, messages, media files, comments and information needed to provide services you request.
  • Order, subscription, billing and payout records, including payment-provider references and payment status. Financial account information is handled through the payment and payout workflow; do not send it through ordinary messages.
  • Technical and operational information used to run and protect the service, such as request, device, session, error, delivery and security records.

How information is used and shared

We use information to provide your account and requested services; host, deliver and publish content; process payments and subscriptions; communicate with you; provide support; and prevent misuse. Public posts and profile details are visible to other people. Messages and private content are available to their intended recipients and people with authorized administrative or support access.

Hosting, communications, payment and other service providers receive information needed for their part of a requested service. We may disclose information when legally required, to respond to valid legal process, or to investigate misuse and protect users and the service.

YouTube and Google connections

Lightek uses YouTube API Services and the official YouTube player. When a channel owner connects a Google account, Lightek uses the granted authorization to identify the selected channel, upload authorized videos, check publishing status and remove videos withdrawn through the publishing workflow. OAuth credentials are stored encrypted in Lightek Vault and used by the authorized publishing service.

A creator who submits a video to the central Lightek channel authorizes a public copy, including its title and description, to be sent to YouTube. The source file remains in the creator’s Lightek publishing space. A public YouTube copy may be seen or shared outside Lightek. Playing embedded videos communicates with Google/YouTube, which may collect device and viewing information and use cookies or similar technologies under the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell Google user data or use Google OAuth credentials or authorized YouTube data to train Nevaeh or another general-purpose AI model. Channel publishing does not grant access to unrelated Google services.

Your choices, deletion and revocation

You can edit available profile settings, remove your own posts using the post controls, manage subscription renewal, and withdraw your own authorized YouTube publication through its publishing screen. Withdrawing a channel upload and deleting an original source are separate actions.

To request access, correction, export, account closure or deletion of information held by Lightek, contact marlon@lightekmcg.com. We may verify your identity before acting. Channel owners can also revoke Lightek’s Google authorization using Google’s connected-app security settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized API use; it does not itself remove a video already published on YouTube. Requests to delete stored authorized YouTube data are handled as soon as possible and within 30 calendar days, subject to applicable YouTube requirements.

Storage, security and retention

We retain information needed to operate requested services and meet security, accounting and legal obligations. Retention depends on the kind of information and service. Account closure may leave records that must be retained for those purposes. Copies already shared with other users or published on external services may remain under those recipients’ control. We use access controls and security measures appropriate to the service; no storage or transmission system is risk-free.

Optional camera, microphone and media access depends on your device permissions. You can change those permissions in your browser or device settings. Session and application storage support sign-in, security, preferences and installed-app operation.

Contact and updates

Contact LightekMCG at marlon@lightekmcg.com about privacy or this policy. We publish changes here with an updated date and provide additional notice where required.
